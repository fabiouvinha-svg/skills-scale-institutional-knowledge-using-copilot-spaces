# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation describes OctoAcme’s approach to managing cross-functional projects from initial idea through delivery and learning. The process is iterative and customer-focused, with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, stakeholder alignment, and shared project artifacts supporting consistent execution.

## Project lifecycle

1. **Initiate and validate** the work: define the problem, goal, success measures, stakeholders, initial risks, and resource needs. Confirm alignment and a go/no-go decision before moving into planning.
2. **Plan scope and delivery**: create a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria and estimates, agree on the Definition of Done, map milestones, and identify dependencies and risks.
3. **Execute and track iteratively**: deliver work in small increments, track progress on a project board, review demos, and keep risks and dependencies visible. Use regular team check-ins to resolve blockers and coordinate delivery.
4. **Release and verify**: meet acceptance criteria, pass CI and security checks, prepare release notes and rollback or mitigation plans, then deploy and verify the release.
5. **Reflect and improve**: hold retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents. Record focused, owned action items and follow up on their impact.

Across the lifecycle, the Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; the Product Manager defines outcomes, prioritizes work, and measures success; developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Communication includes team standups, weekly delivery and PM syncs, milestone demos, and regular stakeholder updates. OctoAcme supports quality with automated tests and linting in CI, unit and applicable integration tests, security scanning, manual QA when needed, and smoke tests for critical flows before release. Risks are recorded, reviewed regularly, and escalated through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when appropriate.
