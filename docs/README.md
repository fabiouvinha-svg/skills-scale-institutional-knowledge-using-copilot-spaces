# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This landing page summarizes the OctoAcme project management approach and links to the core process documents used across the project lifecycle.

## Overview

OctoAcme runs projects with a clear lifecycle designed to keep work aligned to business value, measurable outcomes, and delivery quality:

1. Initiate and validate the problem
2. Plan scope, milestones, and delivery approach
3. Execute iteratively while tracking progress and risks
4. Release with verification and rollback readiness
5. Capture lessons and improve continuously

Across all phases, the team emphasizes clear ownership, regular stakeholder communication, quality gates, and fast escalation when needed.

## Core project management practices

- Customer-first problem definition and measurable success criteria
- Clear ownership for product, project, and engineering responsibilities
- Collaborative planning with scope, milestones, and dependencies
- Iterative delivery with regular demos, updates, and visible progress tracking
- Risk management, stakeholder communication, and escalation paths
- Quality validation before release, including testing and rollback planning
- Retrospectives and action tracking to improve the process over time

## How these docs fit together

The documents are intended to work as a connected set:

- The overview explains the big picture and how teams should operate.
- Initiation ensures the right work is approved and framed with measurable outcomes.
- Planning creates a concrete backlog and delivery plan.
- Execution keeps work moving with regular review, quality checks, and blockers management.
- Risk and communication ensure alignment with stakeholders and timely escalation.
- Release and deployment reduce operational risk before and after production changes.
- Retrospectives turn project experience into concrete improvements for future work.
